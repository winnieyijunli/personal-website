About

I'm Winnie, a brand and communication designer based in Hong Kong. My focus is brand systems — identities, guidelines and templates built to hold up once they leave my hands and land with programme officers, comms teams and partners in the field. Recent work spans international organisations working on migration, development and shared services.

Principles

Systems over one-offs

A brand isn't a logo — it's a set of decisions other people can make correctly without you in the room.

Clarity is the deliverable

If a stakeholder can't explain it back in one sentence, it isn't finished yet.

Design travels

The same idea has to hold up in a PDF, a webpage, a slide deck and a booth at an event — built to move, not just to look good once.
